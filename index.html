How it works: 3 stops

1

Book in 1 minute

Scan the QR code on our flyer or tram poster. Choose your item and a 20-minute slot.

2

Come with your item

A volunteer host welcomes you and checks your item. No skills needed, nothing to pay.

3

Leave with it fixed

Watch the repair, or learn to do it yourself. Your item is ready to wear again.

Can we fix it?

Usually yes

  • Missing or loose buttons
  • Small holes and tears
  • Broken seams, falling hems
  • Simple zips
  • Bag straps and handles
  • Patches on jeans and jackets

Not at this pop-up

  • Leather and fur
  • Shoe soles
  • Electronics
  • Big alterations (new size, new style)

Not sure? Choose “Something else”: a host checks it on site.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Répare Montpellier is a student initiative. Repairs at the pop-up are free.

I can’t sew. Is it for me?

Yes! Volunteers do the repair. If you want, they show you how, so you can do it next time.

How long does it take?

Your slot is 20 minutes. Most small repairs are done while you wait.
